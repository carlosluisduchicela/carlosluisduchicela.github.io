No match in NPPES for that name. Not a dead end. Three options:
  • Check the legal name. NPPES lists the legal name. It is often not the name on the door. “Riverbend Dermatology Associates, LLC,” not “Riverbend Derm.”

An NPI is the 10-digit ID Medicare gives every provider and practice. We check the format and the registry match. A number that only looks right is not enough.

The flow is four states, not nine screens

Each screen moves a practice from one state to the next, and shows which state they're in. The live sign-up is one long form with no progress indicator and no visible state.

AUnknown specialty → waitlisted. Branch set at sign-up and shown in the product, with queue position and next steps. Screens 1 and 9.
BLive specialty → verifying. Email, password, and agreements, then verification status with any outstanding actions visible. Screens 2, 3, 6.
CVerified → onboarding. A checklist in order, with provider details fetched from NPPES where possible. Screens 4 and 5.
DActivated. First prescription sent. Then referral, asked after the first PA, and team invites. Screens 7 and 8.
▶
