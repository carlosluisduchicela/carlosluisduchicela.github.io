biorender · data analysis · jan 2023 – dec 2024

what two years of usage data say about how scientists work

1.91 million signups, every funnel step, segment, and dollar of mrr — read end to end.

the short version. the full analysis and recommendations are linked from the site.

01 · the funnel

the funnel leaks early, not late

89.0%
finish step 1
58.6%
reach step 2
24.3%
complete step 3

three of four users are gone before the product has had a real chance to show value.

02 · segments

the segment decides everything

  • academic leaders finish step 3 at 52.1% and convert at 3.80%.
  • industry leaders convert best of all — 6.55%.
  • academic lab staff finish step 3 at just 18.4% and convert at 1.58%.
  • “other” users convert at 0.92% — the value proposition never lands.

one onboarding for all of these is the problem, not the users.

03 · the signal

an early first figure predicts payment

users who complete their first figure within 48 hours — 28.3% of signups — convert at far higher rates. advanced features compound it: graphing and poster creation track with higher conversion and retention, especially among industry scientists.

activation is not a tour. it is one real figure, fast, then a second feature.

04 · retention

retention is the wound

22%

10.9%
still there at 12 months
$7.3m

the product acquires. it does not yet keep — lab staff and “other” users leave first.

05 · the proof it can be fixed

reactivation already prints money

15.8%

of churned users were successfully reengaged — worth $1.94m in new mrr. the audience is not gone. it is unwritten-to.

06 · what i would do

the bets, in order

  • rebuild onboarding by segment — lab staff and “other” get their own path, not the leaders' leftovers.
  • engineer the first figure — templates and prompts that get a real figure done inside 48 hours.
  • push the second feature — multi-feature users retain; make graphing/poster the week-two habit.
  • run reactivation as a standing program — it already returns $1.94m; aim it at industry and academic leaders first.
  • watch retention monthly, by segment — the aggregate hides every one of these stories.
